# Exit the Maze

![Exit the Maze Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Exit+the+Maze)

## About The Game

**Exit the Maze** is a relaxing and adventurous maze puzzle game where players guide a little girl named Nana through mysterious labyrinths deep inside an enchanted forest.

One day, Nana was asked by her mother to collect fallen fruits in the forest. While gathering fruits, she accidentally wandered into the dangerous maze of an evil witch and became trapped.

Your mission is to help Nana collect fruits, solve maze challenges, avoid getting lost, and find the exit safely.

With many mysterious mazes, relaxing background music, and competitive leaderboard features, the game offers both exciting challenges and peaceful moments of entertainment.

## Story

Nana is a little girl tasked by her mother to collect fallen fruits in the forest. Unfortunately, she becomes lost inside the mysterious maze of an evil witch.

Now it is your responsibility to guide Nana through dangerous labyrinths, collect fruits, and help her escape safely.
